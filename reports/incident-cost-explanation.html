Confidential · Incident Report

Security Incident & API Cost Explanation

Prepared forHeadz Team
Date23 May 2026
SubjectGoogle AI (Gemini) account charges

1 Summary

A higher-than-usual charge of ₹14,500 was generated on the project's Google AI (Gemini) account. After investigation, this was caused by malicious, unauthorized activity — an outside party misused the service's access credentials to run a large volume of automated requests. This charge did not come from normal use of the Headz application by real users.

2 What Happened

In plain terms: the "key" that allows the Headz app to use Google's AI service was accessed and abused by an unauthorized third party. They sent a large number of requests in a short period, which is what generated the unexpected cost. As soon as this was detected, we acted immediately to stop it and secure the account.

3 Current Cost Position

Total charges generated by the malicious activity ₹14,500
Current outstanding amount ₹8,000
Account billing threshold ₹6,900

4 Steps We Have Already Taken

  • Replaced (rotated) all access keys, so the old, exposed credentials no longer work.
  • Changed all related account passwords.
  • Removed unwanted / unauthorized accounts and access.
  • Secured the application so the credentials can no longer be accessed from the public side — closing the gap that allowed the misuse.
  • Contacted the Google support team to review the matter and help resolve the charges.

5 Current Position

The application is live and operating normally right now, with no disruption to users. The vulnerability has been closed and no further unauthorized charges can occur through the previous method. We are actively in touch with the Google support team regarding the outstanding amount and will share updates as the matter progresses.

6 Closing

We took this matter seriously and responded quickly to contain it and protect the project. Please feel free to reach out with any questions.
